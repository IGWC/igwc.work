Friday is Valentines Day, and to celebrate we'll have a Valentines Day Work-in! From 11am-2pm at Wells Library, we'll be writing our ~favorite~ university president some valentines! We suspect that because Pam is so widely hated, she won't get many, so we can swoop in and ask for union recognition. Will it work? No, but it will be funny. Plus, it's an excuse to play with construction paper and build community. There will be bagels! There will be coffee! There will be passive-agressive valentines! We're excited to see everyone there!

There will be our bi-weekly Organizing Meeting this Tuesday at 5pm at Canterbury House (719 E 7th St) or join via Zoom (igwc.work/zoom). This is a great place to get looped in to the organizing this semester! If you want to make sure your department is organized, there will be organizer trainings at every meeting. There will also be dedicated time to work on the projects this semester that will position us to fight for this university. If you are feeling overwhelmed by all the bad news, this is a great space to break out of that pattern and take real, manageble steps to push back.

One small action would be signing the petition for Omeed and Sarah's IU Student Body Presidential Campaign! In order to qualify as candidates and offer a progressive and union-friendly option for an important shared governance position, they need 1% of the entire student body to sign the petition supporting their candidacy. Your signature could make all the difference.
