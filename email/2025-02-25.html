Given that everything is picking up both in our organizing and nationwide, Organizing meetings are now every Tuesday at 5pm at Canterbury House (719 E 7th St) or on Zoom! Like always, these Organizing meetings are the best way to get looped into the organizing this semester: making sure your department is organized, helping with research/writing/events, or helping build resources for fellow graduate students so we can stand up for each other.

If you have skills you can lend, we'd love for you to join our effort! Talk to your Department Organizer or fill out the form at igwc.work/organize!
