Need help with taxes? Come by the IGWC Tax Workshop!

At Canterbury House on March 14, 4–5pm, get general tips from experts, and from 5–7pm get peer-to-peer help!

Navigating taxes as a graduate student is a yearly struggle: fellowships, grants, tuition remission, out-of-state compliexities make the process even harder than usual. This is doubly true if you are also an international student navigating overlapping national jurisdictions!

This year, the IGWC has put together a workshop to both get general tips from experts and peer-to-peer advice from other IGWC members. We'd love to see you there!
