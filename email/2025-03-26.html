General Assembly, Saturday,April 5 at 4pm

Join the General Assembly on Saturday, April 5, 4–5pm at the Monroe County Public Library Auditorium!

At this General Assembly, we'll launch our end-of-semester campaign and develop the IGWC plan to defend our research, our jobs, and our departments. In the face of massive cuts to the NSF, NIH, and federally-funded language programs, existing austerity across the university, and attacks on higher education more broadly, it's more important than ever to stand up for fair conditions both as graduate workers and as students. This end-of-semester push will help lay the groundwork for the next year of organizing to protect the people, research, and labor that make IU what it is.

We'll also be opening nominations for IGWC leadership, specifically the two co-chairs, communications officer, and the finance officer.

There will also be a social at Atlas Ballroom after the meeting to just hang out. We hope to see you there!

Building the People's 2030 Plan for IU

The administration has an IU 2030 plan; the people who really run IU are making one too.

The People's 2030 is a cross-coalition organizing project that brings together students, faculty, and the staff at IU that actually make IU what it is. Building a vision of IU together across coalitions can help protect this university and ourselves in the midst of crises both nationally and locally. In the IGWC Organizing Committee, we've been discussing how to lay the groundwork for a mass movement on campus, and this coalition-building is a concrete step to building power beyond graduate workers.

The first assembly of the People's 2030 is this Saturday, March 29 at 3pm in Ballantine Hall 310.
