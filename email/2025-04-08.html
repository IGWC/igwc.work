Organizing Meeting Today at 5!

This weekend, we launched two major projects that will help protect our funding, our research, and ourselves. First is the "Stop the Cuts" petition, which gathered over a hundred signatures just hours after launching. At this Organizing meeting, we want to make sure every member has been talked to about the petition, and we need your help!

Our security as workers financially, materially, and politically is more important than ever—we need to demand that security of IU.

Sign the petition and join the movement to protect our teaching and research: igwc.work/petition!

Sign the Petition!

The second project is the ICE Emergency Plan, which authorizes a potential strike-action in the event of a member of the IU community being detained by ICE. While we hope to never need to use the emergency plan, at this Organizing meeting we'll talk about the possible contingencies, and how to ensure make sure that every member understands the stakes and the plan itself.

Organizing meetings are very Tuesday at 5pm at Canterbury House (719 E 7th St) and on Zoom (igwc.work/zoom).
