Protest at Sample Gates, Thursday, 12pm

Demand IU take action to protect our peers!

Yesterday afternoon, the Department of Homeland Security revoked the visas for several IU graduate workers and fellow IGWC members.

This is not only a violation of their rights as workers at IU and a threat to the educational mission and values of IU itself, but a direct attack on fellow graduate workers. We're standing up for them, first and foremost.

For emergency resources, visit linktr.ee/btownknowyourrights.

Tomorrow (April 10) at 12pm, there will be a protest at Sample Gates to demand that IU ensure the safety of those impacted by providing emergency academic leave, as well as publicly communicating how these students can complete their degrees.

An injury to one is an injury to all.

Solidarity forever.
