Events this Week! International Town Hall, Involvement Fair, Labor Spring, Statewide Labor Conference

Last week, graduate workers won major consessions from IU regarding the continuation of student status regardless of Visa or SEVIS status. While those meetings are ongoing, more details about that will be sent out in the next several days.

In the meantime, there are several fantastic events happening this week!

International Grad Worker Town Hall
Swain West 119
Wednesday, April 16, 5:30pm

In light of the recent visa revocations and the ongoing negotiations with the university, there will be a town hall for international grad workers to get the latest IU-specific information and support.

The session will cover both IU's recent commitments to it's international students, the process for those impacted by Visa/SEVIS revocations, resources for impacted people, and training for interacting with ICE.

In the meantime, Bloomington DSA has put together a set of resources specifically for international students: linktr.ee/btownrights4intlstudents.

Join the Town Hall via Zoom

People's Involvement Fair
Media School Commons, Franklin Hall
Thursday, April 17, 12–5pm

As part of the National Day of Action for Higher Ed, a coalition of organizations on campus are putting together the People's Involvement Fair to connect organizations, resources, and build a vision of a better IU.

At 5pm, the event will transition into a rally at Dunn Meadow!

Labor Spring 2025
GISB 1106
Saturday, April 19, 12–4pm

This year, the IGWC is bringing Labor Spring to IU with a series of roundtable discussions about labor, university work, labor history, and organizing. Join us in GISB 1106 to hear from those working to improve the lives of Indiana workers and beyond!

Schedule:

  • 12:00pm: Labor in the Midwest
    Roundtable featuring Local Unions, Labor Historians, and Organizers

  • 1:30pm: Lunch!

  • 2:30pm: The History of the IGWC
    Talk by Zara Anwarzai

  • 3:00pm: Labor Organizing during a Crisis in the University
    Rountable featuring Graduate, Faculty, and Staff Union Organizers

Labor Con 2025
West Lafayette Public Library
Saturday, April 26, 10:30–2pm

As part of our effort to build higher education labor power across the state, we've partnered with ROW at Purdue, as well as and GROW, GLDSA a YDSA Purdue to host a summit on labor organizing in Indiana!

If you want to attend, but need to carpool or need support covering the registration fee, email laborcon@indianagrads.org. This is a great space to build labor power and networks across the state.

Schedule:

  • 10:30am: Introductions, Coffee, and Union 101 Presentation 

  • 11:30am: Workshops

  • 12:15pm: Lunch and chat with organizers

  • 1:15pm: Workshops 2 

  • 1:45pm: Closing Remarks
