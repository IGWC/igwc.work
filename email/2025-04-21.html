Know Your Rights Training after Organizing Meeting Tomorrow!

At our Organizing Committee Meeting tomorrow at 5pm at Canterbury House (719 E 7th St) and on Zoom (igwc.work/zoom), we'll be working to make sure every member knows about our current peition to the administration to protect our funding, our degrees, and our rights as workers, especially international workers.

Afterwards at 6pm, we'll host a Know Your Rights training to provide verified information and reccomendations for interacting with police, reading warrants, traveling, and getting in touch with legal support. This is information that is directly useful for both international students and domestic students as well, whether for yourself or for someone you know.

Trainings and Resources Available for Departments

Lots of information is circulating about the "best practices" and guidelines for international students right now. IGWC is collaborating with the Bloomington Alliance for Immigrant Rights to provide the most up-to-date and verified information.

If you want to schedule a training for your department, or if faculty are looking for good information to provide to their students, reach out to BloomAIR@proton.me.

If you just need a quick reminder about your rights, visit linktr.ee/btownrights4intlstudents, especially the "Know Your Rights" presentation.
