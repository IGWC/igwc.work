No ICE in Bloomington: Courthouse Square, Friday, May 2, 6:30pm

Over the last few days, Federal Agents have actively targeted and harassed vulnerable communities living in Bloomington.

On Friday, May 2, at 6:30pm at the Monroe County Courthouse Square, multiple organizations and community members across Bloomington will stand in solidarity with those impacted and demand No ICE in Bloomington.

For guidance and a list of legal support services are available in at linktr.ee/btownrights4intlstudents put together by Bloomington DSA, particularly the ICE/Know Your Rights PDF.

Know Your Rights/ICE Training Materials

Solidarity means that we stand up for each other. If ICE detains a member of the IU community, especially a student or staff member, we'd launch our Emergency ICE Plan: an immediate protest and a pathway for a possible emergency strike-action pending the approval of a General Assembly vote.

For more information or to help organize, talk to your Department Organizer or join our Organizing meetings every Tuesday at 5pm at Canterbury House (719 E 7th St) and on Zoom.

Solidarity forever.
