Special General Assembly Tonight at 7pm

Massive cuts and consolidations at IU are currently slated for Academic Year 2026–2027. These cuts and consolidations not only worsen the quality of education at IU, but drastically worsen working conditions for graduate workers across campus.

These are our degrees, our work, and our campus—this is our university. As workers who make it function, we deserve a say in how it runs.

At tonight's Special General Assembly, we'll talk about the campaign for next year, how we plan to prove we're still the majority, and how we plan to not only save our departments, but improve conditions and pay for graduate workers across campus.

Join us at the all-virtual meeting tonight (July 15) at 7pm: igwc.work/zoom.
