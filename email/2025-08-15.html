Grad Workers Experience Temporary Lapse in Healthcare because of IU Admin

Every year grad worker insurance renews on August 1, but this year, because of a delay in the contract filings, hundreds of graduate workers have lost access to insurance for several weeks. While IU is promising to backfill health coverage during the temporary loss of insurance (reach out to studenhc@iu.edu if you find your insurance is not working), graduate workers have been forced to negotiate between their healthcare provider and IU for basic health coverage included in our contracts.

Letting health insurance for hundreds of graduate workers lapse without any communication from IU administration is just more evidence that IU administration do not have the best interests of graduate workers in mind. This is why we need union recognition. Graduate workers deserve to be able to advocate for ourselves collectively to make sure IU fulfills it's obligations to the workers who make it function. We need a formal collective bargaining agreement with IU—we need union recognition.

If IU admin don't care about their graduate workers enough to provide health insurance on time, they certainly can't be trusted to advocate on our behalf to stop the degree/program eliminations facing IU, or improve our wages to we can afford to live in Bloomington. If we want a better IU, we need to fight for it. Our labor runs IU, this is our university.
