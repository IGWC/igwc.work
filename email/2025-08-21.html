Our University: General Assembly

Monroe County Public Library Auditorium, August 23, 12–1:30pm

This year, we're fighting to stop the degree cuts, protect vulnerable students, and win union recognition and living wage without burdening faculty or degree programs in the face of federal funding cuts. At this General Assembly, we'll endorse this year's campaign and present how we can not only protect departments and students under attack, but make IU a better place for every graduate worker.

The General Assembly is August 23, 12–1:30pm in the Monroe County Public Library Auditorium and on Zoom at igwc.work/zoom.

After the General Assembly, join us at the Bryan Park North Shelter for a Union Social with food and drinks!

Celebrate Labor Day Right: Sign Your Union Card at the IGWC Card Launch Rally

Sample Gates, September 1, 12–1:00pm

This labor day, we're launching our 2025 union card drive! We win when the majority of graduate workers stand together. Our teaching, our research, our work makes IU what it is. We deserve to say how IU runs.

We're fighting to reverse the degree/program consolidations, for a living wage in the face of federal funding cuts without burdening departments or faculty, protections for vulnerable students and a sanctuary campus, and union recognition.

Can't make it to the rally, but still want to be the first to sign your 2025 union card? RSVP at igwc.work/labor-day and you'll receive an email to sign your card!
