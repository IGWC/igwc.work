Over 300 Graduate Workers have signed their union cards!

Join us! Grad workers win big when we stand together.

In less than 48 hours, more than 300 graduate workers signed their 2025 union cards. We're fighting to reverse the degree/program consolidations, for a living wage in the face of federal funding cuts without burdening departments or faculty, protections for vulnerable students and a sanctuary campus, and union recognition. Every single union card bring us closer to a university where the workers have a real say over their conditions.

Even if you've sign a union card in past semesters, be sure to sign this new card!

Sign a 2025 Union Card!

We already know that graduate workers win real material gains when we stand together, including huge raises, then end of exorbitant student fees, healthcare, and other benefits after our strikes. We've also protected international student status in the face of SEVIS revocations this April, and forced IU to honor it's commitments to graduate workers.

We know how to win: strong, mass action and solidarity.
That starts with signing a union card.
