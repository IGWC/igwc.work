Have questions about unionizing as an international student?

Join the International Student Town Hall: Friday, Oct. 3, 6pm

Over a third of all graduate workers at IU are International Students—those are workers who deserve to have their voices heard in the fight for better working conditions, better pay, and a better university. Yet, IU admin have relied the real precarity of international workers on visas to silence and intimidate international students.

Graduate workers deserve better.

Come get dinner and information this Friday, October 3rd at 6pm at Canterbury House (719 E 7th St) and on Zoom (igwc.work/zoom). There will be a delicious dinner from Taste of India. Everyone is welcome, so bring your friends and experience what solidarity looks like!
