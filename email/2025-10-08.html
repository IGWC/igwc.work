History and Sociology are part of a dozen departments with a union majority

A quarter of all graduate workers at IU have signed their union cards this year! Have you?

Just a few weeks into our 2025 card drive, a dozen departments reached union majorities, including History and Sociology! Not just that, but a quarter of all graduate workers have joined the movement for better conditions, better pay, and a safer campus for international students.

A unionized department isn't just about card numbers; it's about building the type of solidarity that can create better conditions not just at the university, but at the department level as well. Moving together means moving stronger.

We want to reach a majority of members by the end of the semester, but to do that, we need all hands on deck! Join our Organizing Committee Meetings: 5pm every Thursday at Canterbury House (719 E 7th St) and on Zoom at igwc.work/zoom.
