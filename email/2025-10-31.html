Grad workers deserve a living wage

What do we mean by Our University: Part 4

MIT estimates that a living wage in Bloomington is $43k/year. IU pays it's graduate workers a minimum of only $24k. That is barely over half of what it costs to buy groceries, pay rent, and take care of ourselves in Bloomington.

IU should pay us more.

IU must provide a path to universal 12-month contracts for SAAs with a living wage for all graduate workers, which according to the MIT cost of living standard is currently $43k/year.
