General Assembly: Tomorrow (Nov 15) at 4:30pm

Monroe County Public Library Auditorium and on Zoom

At this General Assembly, we'll be approving the budget, giving updates on our card drive and campaign for our university, and preparing for upcoming actions to end the semester and escalating into the spring!

Join us Saturday, November 15 at 4:30pm at the Monroe County Public Library Auditorium and virtually at igwc.work/zoom.

As our campaign to sign the majority of grad workers at IU to union cards continues strong into the spring, this General Assembly is a place to consider where our power comes from. A majority of signed cards is important, but it's just the first step. More important is building solidarity between graduate workers so we can stand strong together. The union is strong because of our solidarity with each other.

Our power isn't just that a majority of graduate workers want better pay, better conditions, and more say over the university. Our power is the solidarity we have for each other while we fight for those demands—the knowledge that hundreds of graduate workers have your back, and you have theirs. An injury to one of us is an injury to all. That's what gives us power.

Solidarity forever.
