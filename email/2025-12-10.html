Graduate and Professional Student Government calls for IU to recognize IGWC

The Graduate and Professional Student Government (GPSG), which is the shared governance body for all 10,000 graduate and professional students at IU Bloomington, called on IU administration to recognize the IGWC as the collective bargaining representative for Student Academic Appointees.

The resolution outlines the conditions for unionization according to IU policy HR 12-20, which was implemented to ensure "orderly methods and procedures for the collective presentation of employee-employment interests." The policy affirms the collective bargaining rights of employees in Indiana, explicitly outlining a procedure for collective bargaining with the university to avoid the disruption of a strike or other labor action. In short, the policy establishes a policy for mutual cooperation at the bargaining table.

In 2022, and 2024, graduate workers followed the procedure of HR 12-20 only to be ignored by the IU administration. What followed were two substantial strikes that massively disrupted the university and gave graduate workers a 46% raise and other concessions. Both of those strikes could have been easily avoided by meeting IGWC representatives at the negotiating table. Right now, we are following the procedure for collecting union cards again. We hope IU admin choose to meet us at the negotiating table—but we've seen that we are able to achieve massive victories even when they don't.

This year, we're fighting for secured funding for grads funded through grants, a sanctuary campus, and protection of at-risk degrees, in addition to living wages and collective bargaining.
