Show Solidarity with Minneapolis at Atlas this Friday 6pm

Workers in Minneapolis are prepping an economic blackout to protest ICE violence on January 23

Workers, unions—including our sister union University of Minnesota Graduate Labor Union (UE Local 1105), and everyday Minnesotans are calling for "no work, no school, no shopping" on Friday, January 23rd to demand ICE out of Minnesota and justice for victims of ICE's racist attacks in Minneapolis and the murder of Renee Nicole Good. The day-long economic blackout cuts across multiple sectors, including small businesses and schools, and in many ways resembles a General Strike. Like the General Strike in San Francisco in 1934 when over 150,000 people protested the murder of two striking longshore workers, the mass mobilization to demand an end to state violence has the potential to radically shift the balance of power towards workers.

In solidarity with Minneapolis, we're gathering at Atlas Ballroom this Friday (Jan 23) at 6pm to talk about conditions and socialize! We'll see you there!
