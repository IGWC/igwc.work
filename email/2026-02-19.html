IGWC releases Field Report: A union paper on working conditions and labor organizing at IU

This morning—the first day of classes—graduate workers distributed 500 union newspapers across every major department at IU. The newspaper outlines the rising cost of living, deteriorating working conditions, and funding/program cuts across the university as well as IGWC organizing both past and present to improve the lives of graduate workers.

Articles include a report on the rising cost of living compared to grad worker stipends; the harms and impact of funding, program, and department cuts for current students, academia, and the state economy; the tactics and legal-state of organizing in Indiana; and the strange flow of money through IU Foundation away from students and workers at IU.
