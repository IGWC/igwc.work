Rally for a Sanctuary Campus at IU!

Outside Bloomington Faculty Council Meeting in Franklin Hall, 3/3/26 2:00–5:00pm

Demand IU become a Sanctuary Campus! Join the rally between 2–5pm and get food, play games, and flyer outside of the Bloomington Faculty Council Meeting.

Last April, after threatening to strike if IU cooperated with ICE, grad workers won some of the best protections for international students in the country—ensuring that IU would maintain student status for any student unjustly impacted by ICE. Now, IU needs to take a public stand in support of its workers and students.

A sanctuary campus is a commitment to non-cooperation with ICE and the proactive removal of tools that enable ICE like Flock cameras and other surveillance.Indiana Senate Bill 76 threatens to withhold public funding from schools that do not actively cooperate with ICE. This is a clear threat to students and workers at IU. If IU wants to create a safe environment for research and education, it must oppose this bill and commit to non-cooperation with ICE.

Right now, there are multiple actions IU could take to easily protect its students and workers:

  • Oppose Indiana Senate Bill 76

  • Make cooperation with ICE a code of conduct violation

  • Establish designated secure spaces and campus and implement a system to alert campus members to ICE activity

  • Commit to withhold or remove information about students, faculty, staff, or community members from ICE

  • Remove surveillance technologies (like Flock cameras) and destroy all associated data

  • Reaffirm that faculty have authority over admissions and administration cannot rescind admission based on nationality

  • Launch an assessment to ensure a safe and accessible campus for all students and workers in consultation with students, faculty, and staff

Each of these actions costs almost nothing to implement and would help protect students and workers from ICE. IU admin clearly won't take these steps without workers and students demanding them.

Stand in solidarity, join the rally: Franklin Hall, March 3, 2pm!
