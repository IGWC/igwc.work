Healthcare Info Social (4/4), Pack the BFC (4/7), OC (4/8)

In the last two weeks, IU admin tried to cut grad worker health insurance to justify a raise before walking it back after graduate workers resoundingly rejected the idea.

The very idea that a raise should come at the expense of our benefits is insulting. Making our healthcare worse to give us a raise would be a net zero change: we make more money, but have to pay more for prescriptions, treatments, and basic healthcare. Not only that, based on our estimates, these healthcare cuts would have saved nearly a million dollars after the raise while directly endangering the health of graduate workers.

Since learning at our Organizing Committee meetings that admin were looking to cut our insurance to fund the raise, graduate workers in departments across the university have met with chairs and DGSs—many of whom expressed frustration with the continued austerity for the people who make the university function, while people like Chancellor Reingold and President Whitten make more money than ever. In response to our collective organizing against this issue, admin have walked back the plan to cut our healthcare.

Now, we need to make sure admin follow through on the raise, no strings attached. No healthcare cuts, no department cuts, no austerity.
