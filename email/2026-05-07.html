Co-Chair Candidates

The 2 Co-Chairs of the IGWC set the agenda for the meetings of the Coordinating Committee and General Assembly and participate on the Coordinating Committee.

Eli Beaton

I'm a 5th year in History and Art History studying labor history and architecture. I've been organizing with IGWC since before the first strike in 2022, but when I first arrived at IU, I was terrified to sign a union card, let alone strike. I wouldn't have joined the union at all without organizers in my department taking the time to sit down with me and outline how the strategy could work. Even then, I was still nervous. What helped me feel comfortable and eventually join the organizing efforts was the fact organizers in my department listened and built trust with me.

As an Area Coordinator since 2023, a wide majority of the departments I've worked with have achieved union majorities. As IGWC Comms since 2024, I've shown that I can administer complex projects like the IGWC newspaper, keep on message in high-stakes press interactions, and keep up with the consistent work of running IGWC comms. Most importantly, I'm interested in listening to graduate workers, learning what matters to them, and reflecting that our organizing work.

No one thinks this university is going in the right direction, but IGWC has proven that the only way to make things materially better is strong collective action from the workers. Striking is the only way we've forced IU to improve our conditions beyond the status quo.

As Co-Chair, I believe IGWC should build towards those material improvements. That means prioritizing organizing fundamentals to meet members where they are, building more trainings/political education to help organizers confidently talk with members, and collectively thinking through challenges and opportunities.

Adri Cruz

My name is Adri. I’m a physics graduate student, and I’ve been organizing with the IGWC for four years. I’ve been the grievance officer for two of them and the co-chair for this past year. I believe that we still need to build a stronger foundation so that when we do act, it comes from a place of collective power.

This past year; I spearheaded the effort to strengthen our relationship with international students by developing international student organizers, coordinating international student specific town halls, and maintaining relationships with non-IU immigrant support networks. Within STEM, I’ve made formal sciences a stronger organizing base. Along with Ashish, the Professional Student Government (GPSG) President-elect, I’ve helped build a strategy for union collaboration with GPSG. And as part of the upcoming GPSG Executive Committee, I’m the Community Development Coordinator (CDC) where I’ll be working to help reach departments we haven’t touched yet.

I would be honored to continue this fight and help make our organization stronger than it has been before.

Matthew Jackson

I'm a 3rd year Ph.D. student from Philosophy. I've served in the union as a department organizer and as an area coordinator since I started here 3 years ago. If elected as co-chair, I would like to use my organizing experience to coordinate union actions across campus, and I'm especially interested in working to better involve areas and departments across IU that have historically been less involved in our collective actions.

Ian Spink

My name is Ian, and I am running for co-chair because I want to make IU a better place for graduate students.

I have been involved with IGWC for 3 years now. I started out as a member of the Coordinating Committee during the 2023-2024 card campaign. I was quickly hired as our first self-funded staff person to directly support card signatures and, in the spring semester, agitate for the Bloomington Faculty Council no confidence vote in President Whitten that overwhelmingly passed with 93% faculty in favor.

I am no stranger to organizing or the unusual context where our union lies. We are in a strange gray area where IU can choose whether or not to recognize us, and this complicates our strategies for affecting change. This has been compounded by federal and state turmoil that has further threatened us as workers and the institution we work for.

My contributions to our strategy have been a combination of tried-and-true organizing combined with outside-the-box tactics. We win by building trust and community with other grad students and finding leverage points to push IU to make this place better for grad workers and everyone else.

Financial Officer

The Financial Officer oversees the financial well-being of the organization, including its legal compliance. The Financial Officer creates a budget, issues recommendations to the Coordinating Committee on any expenditures, and approves expenditure requests based on that budget. The Financial Officer participates on the Coordinating Committee.

Chris Bowers

My vision for IGWC’s Finance Officer centers on augmenting the IGWC’s existing fiscal practices with new ambitions. A primary goal is to move the union toward 501(c)(3) nonprofit status, which would reduce our tax burden, increase financial flexibility, and prevent our hard-earned dues from being siphoned off needlessly. I have experience as treasurer within other graduate student professional organizations (History Graduate Student Association), and remain open to the suggestion of our members for securing our shared interests in any way.

Communications Officer

The Communications Officer oversees the communications both internally and externally for the IGWC. This includes overseeing a team that runs the email, operates the social media, writes press releases, creates newsletters, gives media trainings, makes flyers and informational resources, and maintains the website. The Communications Officer participates on the Coordinating Committee

Bryce Greene

Communication - to both members and the community - is the backbone of any organization. As comms chair, I will focus on close cooperation within the coordinating committee to ensure timely and relevant internal and external communications. I have years of experience writing in magazines, producing audio and speaking publicly about issues I am passionate about. I'm confident that I can put these skills to use in support of our shared goals.

Data Officer

The Data Officer ensures that our data remains safe and secure and produces the tools utilized by organizers to address their needs. Data Officer ensures that our data is both up to date and facilitates transition and storage year-to-year.

Coalition Trustees

The 3 Coalition Trustees ensure the financial propriety of the IGWC by overseeing the books and the work of the Financial Officer. Twice per year, they report to the Coordinating Committee whether the books are in good standing, and may make recommendations to the Coordinating Committee about improving the Coalition’s financial practices. The trustees cannot serve on the Coordinating Committee.
